EVERY SATURDAY

More Than Bread.
More Than A Meal.

Every Saturday begins with preparation, continues with fellowship, and ends with gratitude. This is what a Saturday at House of Philia looks like.

SATURDAY OUTREACH

Every Saturday tells another story.

Saturdays are the heartbeat of House of Philia. We wake up early in the morning to prepare pastries, meals, drinks and everything needed to serve our community with excellence.

Every person is welcomed with dignity, conversation, encouragement, friendship and prayer.

A SATURDAY SCHEDULE

From preparation to fellowship.

09:45

Preparation Begins.

We begin preparing pastries, food, drinks and everything needed for the morning's outreach.

10:45

A little prayer.

Every Saturday has to start with prayer to give gratitude to our Lord for this privilege we've been given.

Families, Men, Women, children all com together and line
10:45

Serving begins.

Families, children and members of the community gather together as meals and pastries are shared with love.

Because nourishment isn't only physical.

Moments from our community outreach.

COMMUNITY MOMENTS

Small moments become lasting memories.

Every Saturday is filled with conversation and encouragement. These moments remind us that genuine love is experienced through people.

House of Philia is built on relationships before routines.

A MORNING OF SERVICE

Behind every Saturday is a team serving with love.

From preparing bread to sharing meals and conversations, every volunteer plays a part in creating a welcoming space for our community.

WHAT A SATURDAY LOOKS LIKE

Every act of service begins with intention.

Preparation

We gather early to prepare meals and refreshments with care and excellence.

Connection

Conversations, laughter, encouragement and fellowship create space for genuine community.

Prayer

We pray with and for members of our community, placing every act of service before God.

PRAYER & FELLOWSHIP

Serving the whole person.

At House of Philia, food is only part of the story. We spend time listening, encouraging and praying with people who join us every Saturday.

We believe community grows through presence, compassion and walking alongside one another.

Every person is welcomed with dignity because every person matters.

OUR HEART FOR SATURDAYS

Love is something we practice.

Every Saturday is an opportunity to reflect God's love through simple acts of service. A shared meal. A welcoming smile. A listening ear. A prayer offered in faith. These ordinary moments become extraordinary expressions of compassion.
