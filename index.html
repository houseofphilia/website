WHO WE ARE

More than an organisation.
A family serving a community.

Philia is the Greek word for brotherly love. House of Philia exists to serve the Durban community through meals, fellowship, compassion and faith.

We believe serving is not about standing above people. It is about walking alongside them, sharing life together, and reflecting God's love through genuine acts of kindness.
