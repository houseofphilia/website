WHAT IS PHILIA?

More than food. More than charity.

Philia is the Greek word for brotherly love. House of Philia exists to create a home where faith is lived through compassion, kindness, shared meals and genuine relationships.

We believe community grows when people gather around the same table, pray together, laugh together and care for one another.

OUR JOURNEY

Over a decade of faithful service.

2015

Where it began.

House of Philia began with a simple desire: to serve neighbours through love, food, faith and community.

Growing Together

Every Saturday became tradition.

Families gathered. Meals were shared. Friendships formed around tables and prayer circles.

Today

Still serving with the same heart.

More than 10 years later, House of Philia continues serving over 200 people every Saturday with compassion and hope.

EVERY SATURDAY

A day built around love.

Every Saturday begins before sunrise and ends with grateful hearts. This is what a typical Saturday at House of Philia looks like.

08:00 AM

Preparing with purpose.

Volunteers prepare fresh pastries, meals, drinks and food parcels before welcoming the community.

09:30 AM

Prayer comes first.

Before serving begins, everyone gathers together in prayer, asking God to guide every conversation, every meal and every act of kindness.

10:30 AM

Serving meals. Sharing hope.

Meals are shared with dignity, conversations happen naturally, and relationships continue to grow week after week.

12:00 PM

Leaving with full hearts.

Saturdays end with gratitude, reflection and the joy of serving together as one community.
