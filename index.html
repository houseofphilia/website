WHO WE ARE

More than an organisation.
A family serving a community.

Philia is the Greek word for brotherly love. House of Philia exists to serve the community through meals, prayer, compassion and faith.

We believe serving is not about standing above people. It's about walking alongside them, sharing life together, and reflecting God's love through genuine acts of kindness.

OUR JOURNEY

More than ten years of faithful service.

What began with a heart to serve has grown into a community built on compassion, consistency and faith.

2015

The Beginning.

House of Philia began with a simple mission: To bring the soul to our Creator through serving the community with genuine love and compassion.

Growing Together

One Saturday became many Saturdays.

Week after week people continued showing up, preparing meals, building relationships and serving faithfully.

Today

Still serving with the same heart.

More than a decade later, House of Philia continues serving over 200 people every Saturday.
