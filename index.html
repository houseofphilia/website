WHAT IS PHILIA?

More than food. More than charity.

Philia is the Greek word for brotherly love. House of Philia exists to create a home where faith is lived through compassion, kindness, shared meals and genuine relationships.

We believe community grows when people gather around the same table, pray together, laugh together and care for one another.
