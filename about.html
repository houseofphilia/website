ABOUT HOUSE OF PHILIA

Love that looks like community.

House of Philia exists to serve the Durban community with compassion, dignity and faith. We believe love is something that is lived, shared and demonstrated through service.

OUR STORY

A simple heart to serve became a decade of faithfulness.

House of Philia began with a desire to love our neighbours in practical, meaningful ways. What started as serving meals has become a place of friendship, encouragement and hope for many people in the Durban community.

Every Saturday, volunteers gather to prepare food, pastries and warm drinks, creating an environment where people are welcomed with dignity and genuine care.

Over the years, House of Philia has remained rooted in the belief that serving others is an expression of God's love.

THE MEANING OF PHILIA

Brotherly love.

Philia (φιλία)

Philia is the Greek word for affectionate, faithful and brotherly love. It speaks of friendship, loyalty and genuine care between people.

That is the heart of House of Philia. We serve with people, not above people.

OUR MISSION

Serving with genuine love.

To serve the Durban community through practical acts of compassion, meals, fellowship and faith, reflecting God's love in everything we do.

OUR VISION

Building a community where everyone belongs.

We envision a community where every person feels seen, valued and loved through consistent service and meaningful relationships.

OUR VALUES

The values that guide every Saturday.

Compassion

We meet people with empathy, kindness and dignity.

Generosity

We freely give our time, gifts and resources in service to others.

Faith

Everything we do is rooted in Christ and done to the glory of God.

OUR JOURNEY

More than ten years of showing up with love.

Every year has been another opportunity to faithfully serve our community, build relationships and reflect God's love through practical acts of kindness.

2015

Where it all began.

House of Philia was founded with one simple purpose: to serve our neighbours with compassion and genuine love.

Growing Together

A community built through consistency.

Saturdays became a rhythm of preparing food, praying together, sharing fellowship and serving faithfully every week.

Today

Still rooted in the same heart.

More than ten years later, House of Philia continues serving over 200 people every Saturday across the Durban community.

WHY WE SERVE

Because every person deserves dignity.

We believe that serving goes beyond providing food. It is about creating space for people to feel welcomed, valued and loved.

Every conversation matters. Every smile matters. Every prayer matters.

We serve with people, not above people. We walk alongside our community as neighbours and friends.

OUR FOUNDATION

Everything we do is to the glory of God.

Our work begins and ends with Christ. Every meal prepared. Every pastry shared. Every prayer spoken. Every act of kindness. We serve because we have first been loved by God.

“Whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God.” 1 Corinthians 10:31

OUR COMMUNITY

Durban is home.

House of Philia is deeply rooted in the Durban community. Every Saturday we gather, prepare meals together, pray together and serve together.

Community is not simply where we work. Community is who we are.

We celebrate together, support one another and continue building meaningful relationships that extend far beyond a single meal.
